Carbon fibre rim, Formula Student car

2026 · 5 months

The team's first composite wheel. Unsprung mass is the most expensive mass on the car, and ours was aluminium.

the problem
Every kilogram at the wheel costs more than a kilogram on the chassis: it loads the dampers, slows the suspension response and has to be accelerated and braked at every corner. The team had never built a composite rim, so there was no internal knowledge of layup, tooling or the stiffness the design actually had to hold.
what I did
Led the research and design: laminate architecture, FEM sizing against a camber stiffness target, and the layup and tooling study that made the part manufacturable with the equipment we have in-house.

Aerospike combustion chamber, thermal and structural FEM

2026 · team of 4

Two geometries of a regeneratively cooled rocket engine, and the question of whether either of them survives ignition.

the problem
An aerospike's cooling channels need very thin walls to convect properly, and those same walls have to hold 30 bar of coolant against 2400 K combustion gas. The geometry is only manufacturable by powder bed fusion, so there is no cheap way to test a bad design — the analysis has to carry the decision.
what I did
Four FEM studies in 3DEXPERIENCE — thermal and structural, on an initial and a revised geometry — using quadratic tetrahedral elements on a 1/18 azimuthal sector of an Inconel 718 spike rated for 7.5 kN at 25 bar chamber pressure.
what broke
The wall heat flux refused to converge. Refining the mesh made it climb without limit at the stress concentrator, which turned out to be a geometric singularity created by the jump in boundary condition between the internal and ambient flux zones — the model was reporting an artefact, not a physical value. Measuring 0.15 mm off the singular point converged cleanly under 2%. The number that looked most alarming was the one that meant nothing.
what it didn't solve
The revised geometry is a large improvement and still not acceptable: 1520 K sits above the continuous service limit of Inconel 718 and well above the roughly 1000 K the spike is meant to run at. The residual hot spot is the lower tip, where the film cooling mixes out and the converging section limits conduction to the channels.
tools
3DEXPERIENCE · Inconel 718 · LPBF constraints · Nusselt correlations
2389 K→1520 K peak wall temperature, revised geometry
178→138 MPa peak von Mises stress
75→30 µm maximum displacement

Monocoque inserts and chassis mass, EPFL Racing Team

2023–2025 · two cars

Every load that enters a carbon chassis enters through metal. Inserts are where composite structures actually fail.

the problem
Aluminium inserts carry suspension and restraint loads into the laminate, and pull-out is the governing failure mode rather than anything in the composite itself. They were also over-specified: mass we were carrying because nobody had gone back to check the sizing against test data.
what I did
Designed the critical inserts in CATIA, ran pull-out and compressive FEA in 3DEXPERIENCE, and correlated the predictions against coupon testing before committing to the reduced sizing. Then built the parts — the composite chassis and aerodynamic kit for the team's fifth and sixth cars.
